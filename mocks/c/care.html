What a first conversation is for.

To learn whether in-home support is the right fit, and what a calm week would need to look like. It is not an exam, and it is not a place to send medical records.

  1. The ordinary day

    Personal assistance with routines the household already knows. The pace belongs to the person at home.

  2. A steadier presence

    Visits arranged so the home stays workable. Scheduling is the point of the follow-up email, not a pile of phone tag.

  3. Safety, said plainly

    Practical risks of being at home, named without turning the conversation into a clinical chart.

  4. What this practice does not do here

    Licensed home care and personal assistance. Diagnosis, treatment plans, and medication decisions stay with a clinician. Please leave them out of this form.
