    Monthly records that stay current, for a small business, a nonprofit, or a healthcare office.

    The steps to open, and a calendar of the obligations that follow. Organizing support — not a law firm and not a tax-prep shop.

  3. Calmer operations

    Intake and follow-up that do not depend on remembering the next manual step.
