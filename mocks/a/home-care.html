Rapha Home Care, LLC

Support in the home, held to a quiet standard.

Licensed home care and personal assistance for people who need a steady presence at home. The work is arranged around safety and dignity. It is not a clinical service, and this site does not collect medical records.

What a first conversation can cover

Personal assistance

Help with the ordinary parts of a day, paced to the person and the household.

In-home support

A reliable visit pattern so the home stays workable for the people who live there.

Safety

Attention to the practical risks of being at home, raised early and handled plainly.

Dignity

The baseline, not an add-on. Support should leave a person in charge of their own home.
